About

Backend-first, full-stack in practice

I'm a Java Full Stack Developer with 4.5+ years of experience on enterprise web applications. Most of my work sits in the backend — Java and Spring Boot services, REST and GraphQL APIs, and PostgreSQL — and I build the Angular frontends that consume them.

I care about what happens after the merge: performance, monitoring with Datadog and CloudWatch, and supporting production when something breaks.
